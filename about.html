About

Composition for game and film

Cosmic Empire Productions provides original scoring, arrangement, orchestration, and notation for visual media.

Established in 2020, Cosmic Empire Productions is a composition studio specializing in music for game and film. Services include original composition, arrangement, orchestration, and the preparation of professional scores and parts.

Projects are developed in close collaboration with directors, producers, and creative teams. Emphasis is placed on clear communication, adherence to production schedules, and music that supports narrative and gameplay objectives.

The studio is led by composer Thomas Harris.
