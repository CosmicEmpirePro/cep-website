COSMIC EMPIRE PRODUCTIONS — WEBSITE (FLASH DRIVE)
================================================

This is a fully offline website. No install. No internet required.

HOW TO OPEN ON ANY PC
---------------------
1. Plug in the flash drive.
2. Open this folder.
3. Double-click:  index.html
4. It will open in your default browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.).

For Portfolio audio (large WAV files), prefer:
  Double-click START-PREVIEW.bat
  then open http://127.0.0.1:8770/portfolio.html

Navigate with Home, About, Services, Portfolio, and Contact in the top menu.

CONTACT FORM
------------
The contact form opens a draft in the PC's email app addressed to:
cosmicempireproductions@gmail.com

If the PC has no email app set up, use that address directly instead.

COPYING TO A FLASH DRIVE
------------------------
Copy the entire "Website" folder (keep all files and subfolders together).
Do not move index.html out by itself — css, js, and assets must stay beside it.
