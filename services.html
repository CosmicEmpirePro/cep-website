Services

What Cosmic Empire Productions offers

Four focused services — composition, arrangement, orchestration, and notation.

Composition

Original themes, adaptive layers, and cinematic cues written to picture or to design docs. We shape emotion, pace, and identity so the music supports the project instead of competing with it.

Arrangement

Take a melody, demo, or existing track and rebuild it for a new context — different length, instrumentation, energy, or genre — while keeping the core idea recognizable and strong.

Orchestration

Expand sketches into full ensemble textures. Parts are balanced for clarity, playability, and color whether you are targeting a live orchestra, a hybrid mockup, or a smaller chamber group.

Notation

Professional scores and extracted parts prepared for performers, conductors, and session work. Clean layout, sensible cues, and files ready for rehearsal or recording.

Process

Project workflow

Engagements follow a defined sequence to keep creative direction clear and delivery on schedule.

  1. Brief

    Review project scope, references, technical requirements, and deadlines.

  2. Draft

    Prepare initial cues, arrangements, or sketches for client review.

  3. Revision

    Incorporate feedback and refine material within the agreed revision structure.

  4. Delivery

    Provide final assets as specified — including mockups, stems, and/or notated scores and parts.
